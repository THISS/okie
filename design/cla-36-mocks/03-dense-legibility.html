Dense legibility

Two shots in the pack show the same failure from opposite ends. The 5,000-node stress scene degrades into identical rounded rectangles behind a uniform grey haze of 15,000 edges. The real 1,778-entity self-scan renders nested containers as near-invisible dark-on-dark boxes with sub-pixel labels. In both cases the map is drawing everything it knows and telling you nothing.

The fix is not a better layout. It is refusing to draw what cannot be read, and saying so.

Full details ⇥
  • Draw a budget, not a graphPick a legibility budget in nodes-per-viewport, roughly 30–40 at this card size. Past it, group and count instead of shrinking. Shrinking is how both shots above failed.
  • Counted pucks, not ellipses14 Rust crates · 1,204 symbols is a fact a reader can act on. Fourteen unlabelled rectangles are not. The puck is a real target: click expands it in place.
  • Say what is hiddenThe HUD reads Showing 34 of 1,778. The product already does this well inside isolate mode (Showing 1 of 70) and inside the relationships list — it just never does it for the map as a whole.
  • Promote IsolateIsolate focus is the single most effective consumption tool in the product and it is currently buried inside the story player. On a dense map it should be the first offered action.
  • Edges inherit node rankPeriphery-to-periphery edges do not draw at all. That alone removes most of the grey haze in 08a without touching layout.
  • Missing copy should be quiet“No summary supplied.” repeated nineteen times reads as breakage. Say it once, in the panel, in the voice of a state rather than an error.
