Full details ⇥
  • Three ranks, not oneFocus keeps the summary and the accent. Context keeps its name and a link count. Periphery collapses to a pill. Rank is graph distance from the selection, so it survives any fixture size.
  • One accent per frameThe lime outline, the accent glow, and the primary button all point at the same node. The brand mark, the fixture label, and the story chrome give it up.
  • Chrome demoted below the mapTopbar, rail, and zoom sit on --mock-chrome-text, which is quieter than a periphery node. Nothing in the frame competes with the map for first read.
  • The rail says what it meansL1 Context beats L1. The labels already exist in semanticLensEngine.ts; today they are only exposed to screen readers.
  • Framing follows the graphFit should frame the visible projection, not the root scope. Today it frames system:okie and pushes the context peers off-screen — see the gap between the two images above.
  • Dependencies stop being architectureEight npm packages currently render as full external-system cards at L1. They are provenance, not structure; chips give them back their real weight.
