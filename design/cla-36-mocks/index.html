CLA-36 · design exploration

Okie consumption mocks

Four static frames arguing how to make the Okie atlas easier to read. They are built from the twelve screenshots in design/cla-36-pack, which show the product exactly as it ships on main today. Each frame puts the real screenshot next to the proposal so the delta is arguable rather than asserted.

These are HTML and CSS only. Open any file directly in a browser — no build, no server, no dependencies. Nothing here is wired into the app; apps/web/src/app.css is untouched and the canvas renderer knows nothing about any of it. The written argument lives in design/cla-36-brief.md.

The four frames

  • 1 · A calmer L1 Rank the canvas into focus, context, and periphery. Spend the accent on exactly one node. Push the chrome below the map. Label the level rail.
  • 2 · Inspector vs canvas Three inspector states on one seam — Peek at 232 px as the default, Detail at 376 px on request, Read at 560 px only for source.
  • 3 · Dense legibility Give the canvas a legibility budget. Past it, group into counted pucks and say what is hidden. Promote Isolate out of the story player.
  • 4 · Themes Lime-on-black stays the default. Add a light theme for distribution and turn the accent into a single swappable slot for customer atlases.
